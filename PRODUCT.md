# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering peers, potential collaborators, and portfolio visitors exploring Nii Yeboah's work. Developers evaluating the toolkit are a secondary audience who need a direct path from a convincing example to usable documentation.

## Product Purpose

Dagr is a directed-graph toolkit for the web. Its showcase makes the engineering understandable through a chess opening atlas, an explorable graph of its own architecture, rich content, and observable layout changes. The user approved the chess atlas as the richer flagship on 2026-09-22. It connects Nii's engineering practice with PRNT's generative-art work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, geometric, exploratory. Preserve the existing Dagr mark and the relationship to PRNT's Muslin design system. The user selected direction B, “Inside the graph,” on 2026-09-18.

## Anti-references

Do not lead with the campaign/DND fixture, generic feature-card marketing, fabricated telemetry, or complexity that makes the architecture harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Show working behavior before explaining implementation detail.
- Let visitors explore relationships and inspect meaning.
- Distinguish source-derived architecture, illustrative examples, and measurements.
- Keep current capabilities and planned editor features separate.
- Preserve experiments as artifacts without making them the public showcase.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA in changed surfaces. Use keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable architecture alternatives, reduced-motion support, and labels that do not depend on color or hover alone.
