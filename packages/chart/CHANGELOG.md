# @prnt/dagr-chart

## Unreleased

- New package, private until its packaging checks land. `DagrChart` draws bar
  and line series over a category axis with `@prnt/dagr-render`: one or two
  y-axes scaled by `d3-scale`, marks matched across renders by id so a change
  glides, grows in or shrinks out on the renderer's springs, a GPU-picked
  tooltip, a hidden data table, and reduced-motion support. `layoutChart` is
  the pure layout it draws from.
