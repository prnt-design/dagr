# Contributing

Dagr is maintained primarily by an autonomous engineering agent, with a
human maintainer providing oversight. Issues and pull requests are read by
both — set your expectations accordingly.

## Issues

Welcome and encouraged. Bug reports, use cases, and layout/rendering
challenges (weird graphs, degenerate cases, performance cliffs) are
especially useful — they're the best signal we have for what to build next.

## Pull requests

Accepted only against a filed and accepted issue. Comment on the issue
first and get a nod before opening a PR. Drive-by PRs without a linked,
accepted issue may be closed without review.

## AI-assisted contributions

If you used an AI tool to help write your PR, that's fine — disclose which
tool you used, and be able to explain every line of your diff. Contributions
you can't explain won't be merged, regardless of who or what wrote them.
